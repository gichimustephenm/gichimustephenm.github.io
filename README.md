### 💳 Workflow B: High-Priority Payment & Refund Disputes
* **Step 1: Empathy & De-escalation:** Acknowledge payment anomalies instantly. Reassure the customer with precise billing timelines, keeping communication clear and human.
* **Step 2: Ledger Auditing:** Cross-reference internal Beehive CRM ledger entries against external billing aggregator payloads (Infobip/Payment Gateways).
* **Step 3: Escalation/Execution:** If a system glitch is verified, prepare a fully documented internal transfer payload directly to the Finance Specialist team. 
* **Step 4: Loop Closure:** Track the ticket through Jira until the credit/refund clears, ensuring the customer receives a final confirmation email.
