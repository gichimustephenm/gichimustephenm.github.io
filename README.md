### 📊 Support Quality Architecture (Kano Model Analysis)
To ensure customer care operations surpass standard service level agreements (SLAs), I categorize support performance thresholds using the **Kano Model** framework:

* **Must-Be Needs (Basic Thresholds):** 
  * *Execution:* Seamless ticket routing, accurate profile verification, and **99%+ shift schedule adherence**. 
  * *Impact:* Eliminates basic customer frustration; forms the absolute foundation of operational readiness.
* **Performance Needs (Linear Satisfiers):** 
  * *Execution:* Direct reductions in **Average Handle Time (AHT)** and consistent increases in **First-Contact Resolution (FCR)**.
  * *Impact:* Customer satisfaction scales upward in direct proportion to how quickly and thoroughly a ticket is closed.
* **Delight Needs (Value Adders):** 
  * *Execution:* Deploying predictive troubleshooting protocols and logging deep, structured engineering bug notes within **Jira** before a user asks for an update.
  * *Impact:* Transforms standard service transactions into premium, relationship-driven brand loyalty loops.
